## Product and interface inspiration

- [Novu](https://github.com/novuhq/novu): multi-channel notification workflows and subscriber preferences.
- [Courier](https://www.courier.com/): tenant-aware notifications, routing and investigation logs; product reference, not a claim of an open-source Courier implementation.
- [Brevo](https://www.brevo.com/): approachable campaign, contact and automation journeys; product reference.
- [Dub](https://github.com/dubinc/dub): navigation, dashboards and table presentation.
- [Trigger.dev](https://github.com/triggerdotdev/trigger.dev): execution monitoring and operational investigation.
